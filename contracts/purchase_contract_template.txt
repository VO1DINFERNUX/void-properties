2. PURCHASE AND SALE. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Contract, Seller agrees to sell
and convey the Property to Buyer, and Buyer agrees to purchase the Property from Seller, for the
Purchase Price, payable as set forth in this Contract. The Deposit shall be applied toward the
Purchase Price at Closing. Seller shall convey marketable, fee-simple title to the Property to
Buyer by general warranty deed (or the form of deed customary in the jurisdiction where the
Property is located), free and clear of all liens, encumbrances, and other matters affecting
title, except those Buyer agrees in writing to accept.

3. STATUS OF TITLE. Within the time period required by Escrow Agent, Seller shall cause Escrow
Agent to deliver to Buyer a current commitment for an owner's policy of title insurance (the
"Commitment") covering the Property, together with copies of all instruments referenced as
exceptions therein. Buyer shall have until the expiration of the Inspection Period to review the
Commitment and notify Seller in writing of any matters Buyer finds objectionable. Seller shall use
reasonable efforts to cure any such objections prior to Closing; if Seller is unwilling or unable
to cure an objection, Buyer may, at Buyer's option, terminate this Contract and receive a full
refund of the Deposit, or waive the objection and proceed to Closing.

4. INSPECTION AND ACCESS. Buyer and Buyer's agents, contractors, and other representatives shall
have the right, at Buyer's sole expense, to enter upon the Property during the Inspection Period
to conduct any inspections, tests, studies, surveys, and other analyses Buyer deems necessary or
appropriate, including a physical inspection of the Property and a review of all applicable
records. Seller shall provide Buyer and Buyer's representatives reasonable access to the Property
for these purposes. Buyer shall restore the Property to substantially the same condition it was
in before any such inspection or test. If, in Buyer's sole and absolute discretion, the results of
any such inspection, test, study, survey, or analysis are unsatisfactory for any reason, Buyer may
terminate this Contract by written notice to Seller given before expiration of the Inspection
Period and receive a full refund of the Deposit.

5. DEPOSIT RELEASE INSTRUCTIONS. The Deposit shall be held by Escrow Agent in accordance with
Escrow Agent's standard escrow instructions and applicable law. The Deposit shall be released to
Seller only at Closing, applied toward the Purchase Price, or returned to Buyer in the event this
Contract is terminated under a provision entitling Buyer to a refund of the Deposit. Should a
dispute arise between the Parties regarding entitlement to the Deposit, Escrow Agent may, at its
option, interplead the Deposit into a court of competent jurisdiction and shall thereafter be
released and discharged from all further obligation in connection with the Deposit.

6. LEAD-BASED PAINT DISCLOSURE. If the Property includes residential housing constructed prior to
1978, Seller and Buyer shall execute a Lead-Based Paint Disclosure addendum in the form required
by federal law before this Contract becomes binding, and Seller shall provide Buyer with any
records and reports pertaining to lead-based paint or lead-based paint hazards in Seller's
possession.

7. AS-IS PURCHASE. EXCEPT FOR THE EXPRESS REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES SET FORTH IN THIS
CONTRACT, BUYER ACKNOWLEDGES AND AGREES THAT BUYER IS PURCHASING THE PROPERTY "AS IS, WHERE IS,
WITH ALL FAULTS," BASED SOLELY ON BUYER'S OWN INSPECTION AND INVESTIGATION OF THE PROPERTY, AND
NOT IN RELIANCE ON ANY REPRESENTATION, WARRANTY, OR STATEMENT OF SELLER OR ANY AGENT OR
REPRESENTATIVE OF SELLER, WHETHER ORAL OR WRITTEN, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, REGARDING THE PROPERTY OR
ITS CONDITION, EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH HEREIN.

8. ESCROW, COE, AND CLOSING COSTS. Closing shall take place through Escrow Agent on or before the
COE Date set forth in Section 1.6, or such other date as the Parties may agree in writing. At
Closing, Seller shall deliver a duly executed and acknowledged deed conveying the Property to
Buyer, and Buyer shall deliver the balance of the Purchase Price (less the Deposit and plus or
minus prorations and adjustments) in immediately available funds. Closing costs shall be
allocated between the Parties as set forth in Section 1.10, and otherwise in accordance with the
custom of the jurisdiction in which the Property is located.

9. CURE NOTICE; REMEDIES; ATTORNEYS' FEES. If either Party fails to perform any obligation under
this Contract, the non-defaulting Party shall give the defaulting Party written notice of the
default and a reasonable opportunity (not less than three (3) business days) to cure it. If the
default remains uncured, the non-defaulting Party may pursue any remedy available at law or in
equity, including specific performance. The prevailing Party in any action or proceeding arising
out of or relating to this Contract shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees
and costs from the non-prevailing Party.

10. NO ORAL CHANGES. This Contract may not be amended, modified, or supplemented except by a
written instrument signed by both Seller and Buyer. No oral representation, statement, or
agreement shall be binding on either Party.

11. NOTICES. Any notice required or permitted under this Contract shall be in writing and shall
be deemed given when delivered personally, sent by email with confirmation of receipt, or sent by
overnight courier or certified mail, return receipt requested, to the address or email provided
by each Party in this Contract (or such other address as a Party may designate in writing).

12. MISCELLANEOUS.

12.1 Entire Agreement. This Contract (including any addenda and exhibits) constitutes the entire
agreement between the Parties regarding the Property and supersedes all prior negotiations,
representations, and agreements, whether written or oral.

12.2 Assignability and Novation. Buyer may assign this Contract, and all of Buyer's rights, title,
and interest hereunder, to any third party without Seller's consent, and any such assignee shall
be entitled to all of Buyer's rights and shall assume all of Buyer's obligations under this
Contract. Upon such assignment, Buyer shall be released from all further obligations hereunder to
the same extent as if the assignee had been the original "Buyer" named in this Contract (a
novation), and Seller agrees to look solely to the assignee for performance from and after the
date of the assignment.

12.3 Time is of the Essence. Time is of the essence with respect to every term and condition of
this Contract.

12.4 Governing Law. This Contract shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws
of the state in which the Property is located, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

12.5 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures. This Contract may be executed in counterparts, each of
which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same
instrument. Signatures transmitted electronically (including by email or e-signature platform)
shall be deemed original signatures for all purposes.

12.6 Severability. If any provision of this Contract is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the
remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

12.7 Survival. Any provision of this Contract that, by its terms, is intended to survive Closing
shall survive Closing and the delivery and recording of the deed.
